Boundaries play an important role in helping your inner child experience steadiness, protection, and care.

This free, downloadable guide offers a trauma-informed approach to boundaries that centres safety, compassion, and nervous system awareness. It’s designed to meet you with gentleness and respect for your lived experience.

  • Feel unsure or unsettled when thinking about boundaries
  • Notice guilt, fear, or tension arise when expressing limits
  • Tend to prioritise others’ needs over your own
  • Long for a sense of emotional safety within relationships
  • Feel drawn to inner child healing and gentle self-support
  • A compassionate explanation of why boundaries can feel challenging
  • A trauma-informed reframing of boundaries as protection
  • Gentle practices for exploring boundaries at your own pace
  • Reflection exercises to support emotional safety and self-trust
  • Language and perspectives rooted in care rather than pressure

This guide is part of Inner Child Resources, a space devoted to gentle, trauma-informed support for adults healing early emotional wounds.

The approach throughout this guide honours nervous system safety, emotional pacing, and the understanding that healing unfolds through relationship, compassion, and trust.
